**Regionally Variable Economic Impact Research System**

An observational tool. An extension of the human nervous system, into the human economic systems.

---

Money moves. Where it goes after it leaves your hand is invisible to you, and it's invisible to everyone else too. Not because anyone's hiding it, but because nobody has ever built an instrument that could show it.

RVEIRS is that instrument. A privacy-preserving mesh network that measures how money actually flows (geographically) through a local economy: how much of a dollar spent locally stays local, and how far it travels before it leaks out.

Standard economic indicators measure **wealth**, meaning how much is held and where the totals sit. RVEIRS measures **current**: how much is moving, in which direction, through where.

## What it does with that information

**Nothing.**

It doesn't recommend, rank, shame, or optimize. It reports what's true and stops. The design assumption is that people make better decisions when they can see more clearly, and that deciding *for* them, however well-intentioned, is both presumptuous *and* less effective than making the picture legible and getting out of the way.

This is a deliberate constraint, not an unfinished feature.

## Design commitments

- **Cheap.** A basic node is about $20 in parts. Cost is an adoption barrier, and adoption is the whole game.
- **Low-friction.** A business doesn't restructure, incorporate differently, hire a lawyer, or sign anything. It reports where money it was already spending went.
- **Privacy by arithmetic, not by promise.** Raw numbers never leave your device. The guarantee here is a property of the math, which cannot be revised by whoever ends up on your local mesh.
- **No political prerequisite.** RVEIRS doesn't require agreement about capitalism, policy, or *what* should be done. Only that someone wants see their economy more clearly.
- **Un-enclosable.** Free, open, forkable, and licensed so it stays that way.

## Status

**Early. Nothing is deployed.** The design is thorough, the maths are worked out, and the calculator demo runs. The mesh network itself is built but unvalidated.

## Help wanted

Specific gaps, if any of these are your thing:

- **Mesh.** MeshCore roles are unvalidated. Chunking across a ~150 byte packet budget needs building.
- **Math.** The privacy proof, and the temporal merging problem (deltas vs. cumulative snapshots; rates don't average).
- **Security.** Attack surface review, especially around clustering.
- **PWA dev.** The interface exists as a spec and a rough demo, not an app. Current code is LLM generated from my specs, I would *prefer* a human touch, just literally don't have time to code it myself.
- **Anyone who makes things for an audience.** See below.

Open an issue, or reach out. Rough questions welcome. The docs should be honest about what's unfinished and I'd like to know about it if they're wrong.
